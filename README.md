📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes data from the entertainment industry to evaluate audience engagement, content performance, and industry trends. The aim was to understand how different factors (genre, budget, ratings, audience reviews, etc.) impact overall success, and to visualize key insights with dashboards.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies

Python: Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn

SQL: Querying and preprocessing

Tableau / Power BI: Dashboarding and visualization

Excel: Initial data exploration and pivot analysis



Attributes include: Title, Genre, Budget, Revenue, Ratings, Audience Score, Release Year, etc.

🔎 Methodology

Data Cleaning & Preparation

Removed duplicates and missing values.

Standardized genres and release years.

Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

Distribution of genres, ratings, and audience scores.

Comparison of budget vs. revenue to identify profitable categories.

Trends in audience engagement over time.

Visualization & Dashboarding

Built interactive dashboards in Tableau/Power BI.

Created KPIs to track: revenue growth, top genres, high-rating movies, and audience engagement.

📊 Key Insights

Genre Trends: Action and Drama consistently performed well, while niche genres had smaller but loyal audiences.

Revenue vs. Budget: Some mid-budget films generated higher ROI than large-budget productions.

Audience vs. Critics: Audience ratings aligned with critic ratings in most cases, but some films with average critic scores were loved by audiences.

Yearly Trends: Revenue and production budgets increased significantly in recent years, showing industry growth.
